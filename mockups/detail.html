Carroll County Farmers Market — Celebrating Fall

Public ★ 4.6 (128) · Community ★ — new

18 mi · Sat Oct 3 · 8:00 AM – 2:00 PM · Free admission & parking

Why go

Best dated outing this week for a stroller morning — produce, crafts, cafeteria breakfast. Same campus as Corn Maze (opens Sat 2 PM) and Fall Marketplace indoors.

Age-fit: Excellent for little kids (stroll, snacks). Maze optional later — under 4 free if you try a short loop.

Cost vibe: Free admission; pay for goods/food. Drive: ~15–20 min from Finksburg.
